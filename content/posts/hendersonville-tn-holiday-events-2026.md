**Hendersonville's holiday season kicks off on Thanksgiving morning with HolidayFest's Indian Lake Loop 5-mile walk/run on November 26, 2026. Then comes the Deck the Hulls Christmas Boat Parade on Old Hickory Lake on December 5 and the "A Storybook Christmas" parade and tree lighting on Main Street on December 6.**

Few places do the holidays like Hendersonville and Sumner County. If you live here, have family in town, or are thinking about moving to the lake, here's your guide to Hendersonville TN holiday events for 2026, plus simple tips for house hunting between parades.

## Key Takeaways

- **Thanksgiving Day, Nov. 26:** Indian Lake Loop 5-mile walk/run, 8 a.m. to noon, celebrating its 20th year.
- **Saturday, Dec. 5:** Deck the Hulls Christmas Boat Parade on Old Hickory Lake, 5:30 to 8 p.m., free to watch.
- **Sunday, Dec. 6:** HolidayFest Christmas Parade at 2 p.m. on Main Street, with the tree lighting at Memorial Park.
- **For buyers:** Holiday weeks are quieter, and 37.8% of Hendersonville homes had price drops through October, per Redfin.
- **Rates:** Freddie Mac's 30-year average was 7.28% on Nov. 19, 2026.

## What Holiday Events Are in Hendersonville TN in 2026?

All three signature events are hosted by [HolidayFest](https://holidayfesttn.org/), a nonprofit that raises money for Sumner County charities. HolidayFest says it has given about $3.9 million back to the community over its history.

| Event | Date and time | Location | Cost to watch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Indian Lake Loop 5-Mile Walk/Run | Thu., Nov. 26, 8 a.m.-noon | Streets of Indian Lake | Registration required to run |
| Deck the Hulls Christmas Boat Parade | Sat., Dec. 5, 5:30-8 p.m. | Drakes Creek, past Mallard Park, to Sanders Ferry Park | Free |
| "A Storybook Christmas" Parade and Tree Lighting | Sun., Dec. 6, 2-6 p.m. | Main Street and Memorial Park, 151 E. Main St. | Free |

*Source: HolidayFest event pages, checked Nov. 24, 2026. Plans can change, so confirm before you go.*

### Indian Lake Loop on Thanksgiving morning

The Indian Lake Loop is a 5-mile walk/run on Thanksgiving Day. HolidayFest calls it a favorite "pre-turkey ritual," and 2026 marks its 20th year. Costumes are encouraged, and HolidayFest says same-day registration is allowed. Register through the link on HolidayFest's [Indian Lake Loop page](https://holidayfesttn.org/events/indian-lake-loop/).

### Deck the Hulls Christmas Boat Parade

On Saturday, December 5, decorated boats light up Old Hickory Lake starting at 5:30 p.m. The route starts in Drakes Creek, cruises by Mallard Park, where the judges sit, and continues to Sanders Ferry Park. It's free for spectators and for boat owners who enter. If you love the lake, read our guide to [Old Hickory Lake homes in Hendersonville](/posts/old-hickory-lake-homes-hendersonville/).

### "A Storybook Christmas" parade and tree lighting

HolidayFest's Christmas Parade steps off at 2 p.m. Sunday, December 6, on Main Street. HolidayFest lists more than 80 floats, trailers, dancers, and entertainers, with Santa at the end. The tree lighting follows directly after at Memorial Park.

## Is the Holiday Season a Good Time to Buy a Home in Hendersonville?

For prepared buyers, it often is. Here's why:

- **Less competition.** Many buyers pause between Thanksgiving and New Year's.
- **More flexible sellers.** Redfin data in our [Hendersonville TN housing market update for November 2026](/posts/hendersonville-tn-housing-market-november-2026/) showed 37.8% of homes with price drops and fewer sales than a year earlier.
- **Steadier prices.** Redfin reported a $513,143 median sale price for the three months ending October 2026, up 2.6% from a year earlier.

Rates moved up before Thanksgiving. Freddie Mac's [weekly survey](https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms) showed the 30-year fixed at 7.28% on November 19, up from 7.11% a week earlier. Mortgage News Daily's index had the 30-year FHA at 6.96% and the 30-year VA at 6.97% on November 23. These are averages, not offers.

## How Can You House Hunt Between Holiday Events?

1. **Get preapproved first.** A **preapproval** is a lender's review of your income, assets, and credit that shows sellers how much you can likely borrow. It makes your offer stronger.
2. **Tour on parade weekend.** Drive neighborhoods near Main Street or the lake before or after the events to see the community at its liveliest.
3. **Look at homes in daylight and at night.** Check commute routes, street lighting, and traffic patterns.
4. **Ask about concessions.** With more price cuts, seller help with closing costs or a rate buydown may be on the table.
5. **Plan your closing around the holidays.** Lenders, title companies, and county offices may have shorter hours. Our [winter home buying guide](/posts/hendersonville-tn-winter-home-buying-guide/) covers a year-end plan.

### Which loan options fit Hendersonville buyers?

- **Conventional loans** for buyers with solid credit, with down payments as low as 3% on some programs.
- **FHA loans** with 3.5% down for qualifying borrowers. See our [FHA loan Hendersonville guide](/posts/fha-loan-hendersonville-tn/).
- **VA loans** for eligible veterans and service members, often with no down payment.
- **USDA loans** for eligible properties in parts of Sumner County.
