**A jumbo loan in Hendersonville TN is a mortgage larger than Sumner County's conforming loan limit. For 2027, that limit rises to $1,086,750 for a one-unit home, up $57,500 from $1,029,250 in 2026. Loans above it are jumbo loans, which often need more cash down, stronger credit, and more reserves.**

The Federal Housing Finance Agency (FHFA) announced the 2027 limits on November 24, 2026. Because Sumner County is part of the Nashville metro, it gets a higher "high-cost" limit than most of the country. Here's what that means for buyers shopping lakefront homes, newer construction, and larger properties around Hendersonville.

## Key Takeaways

- **Sumner County's 2027 one-unit limit is $1,086,750,** versus the $855,400 national baseline (FHFA).
- **That's up $57,500** from the 2026 Sumner County limit of $1,029,250.
- **New limits apply to loans delivered starting January 1, 2027,** per Fannie Mae.
- **Some 2026 jumbo buyers become conforming buyers in 2027,** if their loan lands between $1,029,250 and $1,086,750.
- **Jumbo rates were close to conforming rates** this week: 7.40% vs. 7.26% on Mortgage News Daily's index (Nov. 25, 2026).

## What Is the 2027 Conforming Loan Limit in Sumner County?

A **conforming loan** meets Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac rules, including a maximum loan amount. A loan above that limit is a **jumbo loan**. A loan above the national baseline but within a high-cost county's limit is often called a **high-balance conforming loan**.

| Property type | 2026 Sumner County limit | 2027 Sumner County limit |
|---|---|---|
| One unit | $1,029,250 | $1,086,750 |
| Two units | $1,317,650 | $1,391,250 |
| Three units | $1,592,700 | $1,681,700 |
| Four units | $1,979,350 | $2,089,950 |

*Source: [FHFA conforming loan limits](https://www.fhfa.gov/data/conforming-loan-limit), 2026 and 2027 county files. Davidson and Williamson Counties share the same 2027 limits.*

FHFA raised the national baseline 2.72% to match home price growth from the third quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026. High-cost areas like the Nashville metro rose because local median values increased too.

## What Are Jumbo Loan Requirements?

Jumbo loans aren't bought by Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac, so each lender sets its own rules. Common factors include:

- **Down payment:** often 10% to 20% or more, depending on loan size and program.
- **Credit score:** usually higher than for many conforming loans.
- **Debt-to-income ratio (DTI):** your monthly debts, including the new payment, divided by gross monthly income. Many jumbo lenders prefer a lower DTI.
- **Cash reserves:** money left after closing, measured in months of payments. Jumbo lenders often ask for several months.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the jumbo loan limit in Hendersonville TN for 2027?

A loan is generally jumbo if it's above the conforming limit. For 2027, FHFA set Sumner County's one-unit limit at $1,086,750, up from $1,029,250 in 2026. Loans above that amount are usually jumbo loans.

### When does the 2027 Sumner County loan limit take effect?

The new limits apply to loans delivered to Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac starting January 1, 2027. Ask your loan officer how timing affects your specific loan.

### What do I need to qualify for a jumbo loan?

Jumbo lenders often look for a larger down payment, a higher credit score, a lower debt-to-income ratio and several months of cash reserves. Requirements vary by lender.

### Are jumbo loan rates higher than conforming rates?

Not always. On November 25, 2026, Mortgage News Daily's 30-year jumbo index was 7.40%, compared with 7.26% for its standard 30-year fixed index. Pricing depends on the lender and your profile.

### Can I avoid a jumbo loan with a bigger down payment?

Yes. If your down payment brings the loan to $1,086,750 or less in 2027, it can be a conforming loan in Sumner County instead of a jumbo loan.
