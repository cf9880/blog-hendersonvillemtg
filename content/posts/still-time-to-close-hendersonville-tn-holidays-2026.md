## How Many Days Are Left?

From mid-November:

- **December 31:** about 46 days, a more comfortable window if the file stays on track.

[Zillow](https://www.zillow.com/learn/how-long-does-it-take-to-close-on-a-house/), citing a 2026 ICE study, reports an average close of about 37 days. That is a national figure, not a Sumner County promise. USDA, VA, or jumbo files, or a delayed appraisal, can take longer.

If you already have a contract, ask your loan officer whether the date still works after Thanksgiving and Christmas closures are mapped. If you are just starting, treat year-end as the goal.

## Why Winter and Holidays Need Extra Room Here

In Hendersonville, a year-end close can collide with:

- **Colder inspections and walk-throughs**, including heat, pipes, and roof issues that matter more in Middle Tennessee than they do on the Gulf. Our [Hendersonville winter home buying guide](/posts/hendersonville-tn-winter-home-buying-guide/) covers those property checks.
- **Appraisers and title offices** taking time off around the holidays.
- **The three-business-day Closing Disclosure wait.** The [CFPB](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/when-do-i-get-a-closing-disclosure-en-179/) requires you to receive the form at least three business days before closing. Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day do not count.

Some families also prefer to move during winter break so a job or school change lines up with January. That is a personal scheduling choice, not a reason to pick an unrealistic closing date. If January 2 is safer than December 23, say so in the contract.
