## How to choose a mortgage company in Hendersonville: checklist

1. **Check your credit** and decide on a comfortable payment, including taxes and insurance.
2. **Get three quotes the same day** with the same loan amount, down payment, and lock period.
3. **Compare Loan Estimates** line by line ([CFPB](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/owning-a-home/loan-estimate/)).
4. **Verify licenses** on [NMLS Consumer Access](https://www.nmlsconsumeraccess.org/).
5. **Ask your agent** which lenders communicate well and close on time.
6. **Get fully preapproved** before you tour seriously. In a market with about 4 months of supply, a strong preapproval still matters.
7. **Plan for extras** like HOA dues, lake dock permits, or new-construction timelines.

### Questions to ask a Hendersonville mortgage lender

- Which loan fits my goals, and what would my payment be?
- What are the rate, APR, and total lender fees?
- How long is the rate lock, and can it be extended for new construction?
- Do you offer down payment assistance options?
- Have you financed lake homes or homes with docks?
- How will you keep me updated, and how do I reach you after hours?

## Refinancing in Hendersonville: what homeowners should know

Freddie Mac's 30-year average was **7.32%** on Oct. 22, 2026, up from 6.19% a year earlier ([Freddie Mac PMMS](https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms)). For many Hendersonville homeowners, a refinance just to lower the rate won't pay off right now. It can still make sense to:

- **Drop FHA mortgage insurance** by moving to a conventional loan once you have enough equity. Rising Sumner County values may help.
- **Take cash out** for a renovation, a pool, or to pay off higher-interest debt.
- **Switch from an adjustable rate** to a fixed rate.
- **Use a HELOC** (home equity line of credit) to tap equity while keeping your current first mortgage.

**Break-even point (definition):** total closing costs divided by monthly savings. Ask us for a free rate check and we'll compare a refinance, a HELOC, or keeping your loan as is.
