**Can veterans buy a home in Hendersonville, TN with no money down?** Yes. Eligible veterans, service members, and some surviving spouses can use a VA loan to buy a primary residence in Hendersonville with **$0 down and no monthly mortgage insurance**. Borrowers with full entitlement have no VA loan limit, though the lender still has to approve the loan and the appraisal must support the price.

Veterans Day is a great time to talk about one of the most valuable benefits veterans earn. Here's how a VA loan in Hendersonville, TN works in 2026, what it costs, and how to celebrate the holiday locally.

## Key takeaways

- VA loans allow **no down payment** and have **no monthly mortgage insurance**.
- With **full entitlement**, VA sets **no loan limit**. Your lender decides how much you can afford.
- The 2026 **VA funding fee** for first-time use with under 5% down is **2.15%** of the loan. Veterans receiving VA disability compensation are generally **exempt**.

## What is a VA loan?

A **VA loan** is a mortgage from a private lender that is partly guaranteed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs. Because VA backs part of the loan, lenders can offer terms that are hard to match: no down payment in most cases, no monthly mortgage insurance, and often competitive rates.

### Who qualifies?

Eligibility depends on service history. It generally covers veterans, active-duty service members, National Guard and Reserve members who meet service requirements, and certain surviving spouses. You'll need a **Certificate of Eligibility (COE)**, which we can help you request.

### What is VA entitlement?

**Entitlement** is the amount VA promises to repay your lender if the loan defaults. According to the [VA's entitlement page](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/loan-limits/), if you have full entitlement, you don't have a loan limit, as long as you can afford the loan and the appraisal supports the price. If part of your entitlement is tied up in another VA loan, county limits can affect how much you can borrow with no money down.

## How much is the VA funding fee in 2026?

The **funding fee** is a one-time charge that helps keep the program running. You can pay it at closing or roll it into the loan. The [VA's current chart](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/funding-fee-and-closing-costs/) for purchase loans:

| Down payment | First use | After first use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Less than 5% | 2.15% | 3.3% |
| 5% or more | 1.5% | 1.5% |
| 10% or more | 1.25% | 1.25% |

You generally **don't pay a funding fee** if you receive VA compensation for a service-connected disability, among other exemptions listed by VA.

### Can the seller help with closing costs?

Yes. VA lets sellers pay a buyer's closing costs, but **seller concessions** (extras like paying the funding fee or paying off buyer debts) are capped at **4%** of the home's reasonable value, per VA.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do you need a down payment for a VA loan in Hendersonville, TN?

In most cases, no. Eligible borrowers with enough entitlement can buy a primary residence with $0 down, subject to lender approval and the appraisal.

### Is there a VA loan limit in Sumner County?

Not if you have full entitlement. VA sets no loan limit for full-entitlement borrowers, though your lender still decides how much you can afford.

### How much is the VA funding fee?

For a first-time purchase with under 5% down, it's 2.15% of the loan amount. It drops to 1.5% with 5% down and 1.25% with 10% down.

### Who is exempt from the VA funding fee?

Veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability are generally exempt, along with several other groups listed by VA.

### When is the Hendersonville Veterans Day Parade?

Sunday, November 8, 2026, at 2:00 p.m., from Cherokee Drive to Executive Park Drive, according to the Hendersonville Chamber of Commerce.

### Does a VA loan have mortgage insurance?

No. VA loans don't charge monthly mortgage insurance, which can lower your monthly payment compared with other low-down-payment loans.
