**Hendersonville, Tennessee traces its history to 1784, when surveyor Daniel Smith settled his family on the Cumberland River and began building Rock Castle, now a state historic site. The town is named for Captain William Henderson, its first postmaster in 1801, and it was incorporated as a city in 1969.**

If family is coming to town for Thanksgiving next week, a little local history makes for a great outing. And if you're thinking about moving to Hendersonville, knowing its story helps you see why so many people choose the "City by the Lake." Here's a short guide.

## Key Takeaways

- **Rock Castle** was built from limestone quarried on site, starting in 1784 and finished by 1796 (Tennessee Encyclopedia).
- **Daniel Smith** was a Revolutionary War officer, surveyor, territorial secretary and U.S. senator.
- **Captain William Henderson** gave the town its name and became first postmaster in 1801 (Tennessee Historical Commission marker).
- **Hendersonville became a city** in July 1969 and began operating August 1, 1969 (City of Hendersonville).
- **Old Hickory Lake** gives the city about 26 miles of shoreline.

## What Is Rock Castle?

Rock Castle sits on the shore of Old Hickory Lake, off Indian Lake Road. According to the [Tennessee Historical Commission](https://www.tn.gov/historicalcommission/state-programs/state-historic-sites/rock-castle.html), Daniel Smith and his wife, Sarah Michie Smith, brought their family to the site in 1784 and began construction. Because Daniel was often away surveying and serving in public office, Sarah supervised much of the building and ran the plantation.

The [Tennessee Encyclopedia](https://tennesseeencyclopedia.net/entries/rock-castle/) says workers used limestone quarried on the property and lumber cut from its timber. The house was built in stages and finished by 1796. It blends Georgian and Federal styles, and its two-story portico was added later. The plantation once covered 3,140 acres. Today the site is 18 acres, with the house, a family cemetery and a smokehouse.

The site's story also includes the enslaved people whose labor sustained the property and the sharecroppers who worked the land after emancipation, as the Friends of Rock Castle note in their history of the site.

The State of Tennessee bought the property in 1969. Friends of Rock Castle was chartered in 1971, and Historic Rock Castle opened to the public on May 1, 1981. It is listed on the National Register of Historic Places. Check the site's current hours before you visit, especially around Thanksgiving.

## Who Was Daniel Smith?

Daniel Smith (1748-1818) was one of early Tennessee's most important leaders. The Tennessee Encyclopedia notes that he:

- Produced the first map showing the "Tennessee Territory" in relation to Virginia and Kentucky
- Was appointed by President George Washington in 1790 as secretary of the Territory South of the River Ohio
- Chaired the committee that drafted Tennessee's first constitution and bill of rights in 1796
- Served in the U.S. Senate, first briefly in 1798-99 and again from 1805 until 1809

He died at Rock Castle in 1818.

## Who Was Hendersonville Named After?

The city's namesake is **Captain William Henderson** (1752-circa 1807). A [Tennessee Historical Commission marker](https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=149954) on West Main Street says he was a Revolutionary soldier born in Virginia who moved to Sumner County with his wife, Lockey Trigg, in the late 1790s. He became the community's first postmaster in 1801, and two of his sons held the job after him.

## How Did Hendersonville Grow Into a City?

For most of its history, Hendersonville was a small community. Two big changes shaped the city we know today:

- **Old Hickory Lake.** The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers' Old Hickory Dam on the Cumberland River created the lake in the 1950s. The city now has about 26 miles of shoreline.
- **Incorporation.** Hendersonville was incorporated in July 1969 and began operating as a city on August 1, 1969, according to the [City of Hendersonville](https://www.hvilletn.org/370/City-History).

The Hendersonville Chamber of Commerce says the city has become the largest in Sumner County. Many well-known musicians have called it home, including Johnny Cash and June Carter Cash and Roy Orbison.
