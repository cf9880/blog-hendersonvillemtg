## Is Winter a Good Time to Buy a Home in Hendersonville, TN?

Many buyers think so. School schedules, travel, and colder weather keep a lot of shoppers home. Sellers who list in December usually have a reason to move, such as a new job or a home they already bought.

Nationally, Redfin found that sellers outnumbered buyers by 45% in December 2025. That was the largest gap since its records began in 2013. Freddie Mac's [guide to buying in winter](https://myhome.freddiemac.com/blog/homebuying/buy-or-sell-in-winter) also points out that off-season buyers may see less competition.

A winter tour is useful in another way. You get to see how a home holds heat, how the yard drains, and how the roads feel on a cold morning. If this is your first purchase, start with our [first-time home buyer guide for Hendersonville](/posts/first-time-home-buyer-hendersonville-tn/).

## How Do Today's Mortgage Rates Affect a Winter Purchase?

On October 26, 2026, [Mortgage News Daily](https://www.mortgagenewsdaily.com/mortgage-rates/mnd) reported these averages:

- 30-year fixed: **7.33%**
- 15-year fixed: **6.99%**
- FHA: **6.96%**
- VA: **6.98%**
- Jumbo: **7.60%**

The 30-year average was 1.14 points higher than a year earlier. Freddie Mac's [Primary Mortgage Market Survey](https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms) showed 7.32% for October 22.

At these rates, every dollar of seller credit counts. A credit can pay for closing costs or buy down your rate. With area prices near or above $440,000, compare conforming and jumbo limits early if you're shopping lake homes.

## What Can Buyers Negotiate in a Hendersonville Winter?

When a home has been listed for weeks, you can ask for more than a lower price:

- **Seller credits** for closing costs or a rate buydown
- **Heating system service** or replacement of an aging furnace or heat pump
- **Repairs** to roofs, gutters, or pipes found during inspection
- **A home warranty** for the first year
- **A flexible closing date** around school breaks or a job start

Tour with winter in mind. Ask how old the HVAC system is, look under sinks and in crawl spaces for old freeze damage, and check that outdoor faucets are protected.

## How Do You Close Before the End of the Year?

1. **Get a full preapproval** with income and assets reviewed.
2. **Be under contract by about late November.** Financed deals often take 30 to 45 days.
3. **Book the inspection and appraisal right away.** Holiday weeks fill up fast.
4. **Get homeowners insurance quotes** in the first week.
5. **Expect closures** on Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day.
6. **Confirm wire instructions by phone** with your title company before sending money.

## What Is There to Do in Hendersonville This Holiday Season?

[HolidayFest](https://www.holidayfest.org/), a local nonprofit running since 2007, hosts several favorites:

- **Indian Lake Loop 5-mile walk/run:** Thanksgiving morning, November 26, at 8 a.m.
- **Deck the Hulls Christmas Boat Parade:** Saturday, December 5, 5:30 to 8 p.m. on Old Hickory Lake. Boats travel from Drakes Creek past Mallard Park to Sanders Ferry Park. It's free to watch.
- **Hendersonville Christmas Parade and Tree Lighting:** Sunday, December 6, starting at 2 p.m. on Main Street, with the tree lighting at Memorial Park.

Nearby, the [Gallatin Christmas Parade](https://gallatintn.org/gallatin-christmas-parade/) rolls on Saturday, December 12, at noon.

**History note:** Hendersonville is named for Captain William Henderson, a Revolutionary War soldier who became the community's first postmaster in 1801, according to a local historical marker. The city itself is much younger. It was incorporated in July 1969 and began operating on August 1, 1969, according to the [City of Hendersonville](https://www.hvilletn.org/370/City-History).
