**The Hendersonville TN housing market in November 2026 is cooling but not falling. Redfin reports a median sale price of $513,143 for the three months ending October 2026, up 2.6% year over year, while sales fell 12.1% and more sellers cut prices. Across greater Nashville, inventory is up and pending sales are down, giving buyers more leverage.**

Every fall we look at the numbers for Hendersonville and Sumner County. This update covers the newest October 2026 data, released in November, and what it means if you plan to buy or sell around Old Hickory Lake before or after the holidays.

## Key Takeaways

- **Hendersonville median:** $513,143 (three months ending October 2026), up 2.6% year over year, per Redfin.
- **Fewer sales:** 242 Hendersonville homes sold in October, down from 275 a year earlier.
- **More price cuts:** 37.8% of Hendersonville homes had price drops, up 6.6 points.
- **Nashville area:** 2,540 closings in October, down 12%, with more inventory and fewer pending sales (Greater Nashville REALTORS®).
- **Rates:** Freddie Mac's 30-year average was 7.11% on November 12, 2026.

## What Do the Hendersonville Numbers Show?

According to [Redfin's Hendersonville housing market page](https://www.redfin.com/city/8509/TN/Hendersonville/housing-market), as of October 2026:

| Metric (Hendersonville) | Latest | Year-over-year |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Median sale price (3 months) | $513,143 | +2.6% |
| Homes sold in October | 242 | -12.1% |
| Median days on market | 65 | +4 days |
| Sale-to-list price | 98.4% | -0.12 pt |
| Homes sold above list | 12.7% | -2.6 pt |
| Homes with price drops | 37.8% | +6.6 pt |

Prices are still edging up, but homes are taking longer to sell and more sellers are adjusting. Redfin rates Hendersonville "somewhat competitive," with typical homes selling about 2% below list.

## How Does Sumner County Compare?

[Redfin's Sumner County page](https://www.redfin.com/county/2627/TN/Sumner-County/housing-market) shows a three-month median sale price of **$419,402** through October 2026, down 3.7% year over year, with 271 homes sold in October (down 13.6%) and a 67-day median time on market.

[Greater Nashville REALTORS®](https://www.greaternashvillerealtors.org/market-data-quarterly) reports county data quarterly. For the third quarter of 2026 (July to September), Sumner County had 906 residential closings at a **$475,000** residential median price.

Why the different medians? Redfin and Greater Nashville REALTORS® use different time periods and property mixes. Compare each source with itself over time rather than mixing them. Our [fall 2026 Sumner County report](/posts/sumner-county-housing-market-fall-2026/) has earlier figures for comparison.

## What Is Happening Across the Nashville Area?

[Greater Nashville REALTORS® reported on November 9, 2026](https://www.greaternashvillerealtors.org/news/housing-market-sees-more-inventory-fewer-sales-in-october) that the nine-county region, which includes Sumner County, had:

- **2,540 closings** in October, down 12% from 2,886 a year earlier.
- **1,805 pending sales**, down from 2,276.
- **A $499,990 residential median**, compared with $515,000 last October.
- **61 days** from list to contract for single-family homes.

"The housing market continued to shift toward more favorable conditions for buyers in October, as inventory increased and both pending and closed sales declined," said Greater Nashville REALTORS® President Jack Gaughan.

Nationally, the [National Association of REALTORS®](https://www.nar.realtor/newsroom/existing-home-sales-decreased-5-5-percent-october-nar-report) reported existing-home sales at a 3.75 million annual pace in October 2026, the lowest since August 2010, with a 5.1-month supply.

## How Are Mortgage Rates Affecting Hendersonville Buyers?

Rates are the main reason the market is slower. [Freddie Mac](https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms) put the 30-year fixed average at **7.11%** on November 12, 2026, down for a fourth straight week but up from 6.24% a year earlier. Mortgage News Daily's daily index was **7.51%** for a top-tier 30-year scenario on November 16, 2026, its highest in more than a month.

Higher rates mean a larger payment for the same price, but they also mean less competition. Buyers who are ready now may find more room to ask for repairs, closing cost help or a better price.
