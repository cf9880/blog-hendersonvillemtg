## Why Does the Sumner County Loan Limit Matter?

For 2026, FHFA set a higher conforming limit in Sumner County than the national baseline. The one-unit limit is **$1,029,250**, compared with **$832,750** in many other counties. That means some purchases that would be jumbo elsewhere may still be conforming here.

The limit should still be confirmed on the specific property and loan amount. A preapproval that ignores the local limit, or uses an outdated figure, can confuse a listing agent. We write the letter to match the current FHFA limit and the program the buyer actually fits.
