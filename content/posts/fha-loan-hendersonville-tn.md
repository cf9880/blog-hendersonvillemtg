## What Are the FHA Requirements in Tennessee?

FHA rules are national, but lenders can add their own requirements, called overlays. The basics from HUD:

- **Credit score:** 580 or higher for 3.5% down; 500 to 579 generally needs 10% down. Many lenders set a higher minimum.
- **Down payment:** Can come from savings or an acceptable gift from a family member.
- **Debt-to-income ratio:** Lenders review your total monthly debts against your income, and some approvals allow higher ratios with strong compensating factors.
- **Occupancy:** You must live in the home as your primary residence.
- **Property condition:** The FHA appraisal checks that the home meets HUD's minimum property standards for safety and soundness.
- **Seller help:** Sellers can contribute up to 6% of the sales price toward eligible closing costs.

## How Much Does FHA Mortgage Insurance Cost?

FHA loans have two mortgage insurance premiums (MIP):

1. **Upfront MIP:** 1.75% of the base loan amount, usually added to the loan.
2. **Annual MIP:** Paid monthly. For most 30-year loans with less than 5% down and a base loan of $726,200 or less, the rate is 0.55% per year.

If you put less than 10% down, annual MIP generally lasts for the life of the loan. With 10% or more down, it typically ends after 11 years. Many FHA borrowers later refinance into a conventional loan once they have enough equity.

**Good news for 2026:** Mortgage insurance premiums, including FHA MIP, are tax-deductible again starting in 2026 for itemizers below certain income limits, according to the IRS. Ask your tax preparer whether you qualify.

## Is FHA or Conventional Better in Hendersonville?

It depends on your credit and down payment:

- **FHA often fits** buyers with scores in the 580s to 600s, higher debt ratios, or recent credit bumps.
- **Conventional often fits** buyers with stronger credit, because private mortgage insurance can cost less and can be removed once you reach enough equity.
