Hendersonville has grown from a tiny new city into one of the biggest suburbs in Middle Tennessee. This first-time home buyer guide for Hendersonville, TN covers the steps, the loan options, the local numbers, and a little hometown history along the way.

## From 262 residents to more than 64,000

Hendersonville's story is a lesson in how fast a place can grow. According to the [Hendersonville Chamber of Commerce](https://www.hendersonvillechamber.com/area-information-relocation/), surveyor Daniel Smith settled the area in 1784, and William Henderson, the town's namesake, arrived in 1790. Yet Hendersonville didn't become a city until **1969**, when it formed with just **262 residents**. That made it the smallest city in Tennessee at the time, per the Chamber's city guide.

Then Old Hickory Lake and Nashville's growth changed everything. The Chamber's guide says Hendersonville was the fastest-growing city in the nation during the 1970s. The U.S. Census Bureau estimated its population at **64,266** as of July 1, 2025.

### Places first-time buyers should explore

- **Historic Rock Castle:** Begun in 1784 and called the oldest house in Middle Tennessee, it's now a state historic site on Old Hickory Lake.
- **Monthaven:** Built around 1860 and used as a field hospital during the Civil War, it now houses the Monthaven Arts & Cultural Center.
- **The lake itself:** Parks, marinas, and boat ramps line Old Hickory Lake. See our [Old Hickory Lake homes guide](../old-hickory-lake-homes-hendersonville/).

## What are the steps to buying your first home?

1. **Check your credit and budget.** Know your score and how much you're comfortable paying each month, including taxes and insurance.
2. **Get preapproved.** A lender reviews your income, assets, debts, and credit, then tells you how much you may borrow.
3. **Pick your loan program.** Compare down payment, mortgage insurance, and rate options (see below).
4. **Shop for homes.** Work with a local real estate agent who knows Hendersonville neighborhoods and schools.
5. **Make an offer and get inspected.** Today's market gives buyers more room to ask for repairs or seller-paid closing costs.
6. **Appraisal, underwriting, and closing.** Your loan is finalized, and you get the keys.

## Which loan is best for a first-time buyer in Hendersonville?

There's no single best loan. It depends on your credit, savings, and plans. Here are the main options Coastal Funding offers for Tennessee primary residences.

### Conventional loans

A **conventional loan** isn't backed by a government agency. Qualified first-time buyers can put down as little as 3%. With less than 20% down, you'll usually pay **private mortgage insurance (PMI)**, which can typically be removed once you build enough equity.

### FHA loans

An **FHA loan** is insured by the Federal Housing Administration. It allows **3.5% down** with a qualifying credit score and is often more flexible about credit history. FHA loans carry mortgage insurance premiums. Mortgage News Daily's index had a 30-year FHA rate at **7.02%** on October 20, 2026.

### VA loans

Eligible veterans and service members can often buy with **$0 down** and no monthly mortgage insurance. Read our full [VA loan guide for Hendersonville](../va-loan-hendersonville-tn/).

### USDA loans

A **USDA loan** offers $0 down for eligible buyers in eligible rural and suburban areas, with household income limits. Many addresses inside larger cities don't qualify, but some areas elsewhere in Sumner County may. Check any address on the [USDA eligibility map](https://eligibility.sc.egov.usda.gov/eligibility/welcomeAction.do).

## Smart money tips for first-time buyers

- **Save for more than the down payment.** Plan for closing costs, moving costs, and a cushion for repairs.
- **Don't open new credit** during the loan process. New debt can change your approval.
- **Ask about seller credits.** With homes sitting longer, sellers may help with closing costs.
- **Look at the full monthly payment:** principal, interest, taxes, insurance, mortgage insurance, and any HOA dues.
