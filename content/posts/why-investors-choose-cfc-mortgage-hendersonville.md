## How Does a DSCR Loan Work in Tennessee?

**DSCR = monthly rent ÷ monthly PITIA** (principal, interest, taxes, insurance, and association dues). A ratio of 1.0 means the rent covers the payment. Many lenders look for 1.0 or higher.

Coastal Funding offers DSCR loans in 40+ states, including Tennessee. These are generally non-QM investment-property loans. Lenders still review credit, assets, the property, and rent support such as a lease or an appraiser's rent schedule.
